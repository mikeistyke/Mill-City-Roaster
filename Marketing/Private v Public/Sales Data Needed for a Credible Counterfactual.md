# Sales Data Needed for a Credible Counterfactual
# No credible counterfactual exists that I know of

Even with all of this data, one roaster can only be compared to a modeled estimate of what it would have sold for. It can't be run as a true controlled test.

## Comparable sales

For each MCR-10 sale, ideally 20 or more:

- Model, build year, serial origin (imported or built in Minneapolis)
- Condition: roast count or hours, never fired or used, crated or not
- Included items: chaff collector, venting, software, accessories
- Asking price and any price changes, with dates
- Final price the buyer paid
- What the seller actually received after fees
- Broker or platform fee in dollars
- Sales channel: CoffeeTec, other dealer, eBay, private sale or manufacturer
- Date listed, date of first serious inquiry, date sold
- Seller location and buyer location
- Freight cost and who paid it, crating cost and who paid it
- Financing used (yes or no)
- Inspection deposit paid, and whether the deal fell through and was relisted

## Market conditions

- New MCR-10 or M-Series list price on each sale date
- Number of competing used 10 kg listings at the time
- Seasonality and interest rates or financing costs on each sale date

## Your private campaign

- Ad spend by channel, impressions, clicks, cost per click
- Site visits by source, separating ad traffic from organic and referral traffic
- Inquiries by source, with each buyer's budget, timing and site readiness
- Qualified leads, inspections, offers and the price of each offer
- Accepted price, freight cost, transaction cost and seller hours
- Days to deposit and days to delivery

## Broker comparison

- CoffeeTec's written quote: the listing price they would set for your unit, your payout and the fee
- The days-to-sale they expect
- Buyer-paid freight versus your included freight, converted to a total delivered cost

## Method requirements

- The same unit of comparison throughout: seller net proceeds and buyer delivered cost
- Pricing adjustments for age, condition, included items and freight
- The time frame and outcome measure defined before launch
- A broker deadline set in advance, so the private result is judged against an agreed alternative

Most of the comparable-sales data, especially final prices, fees and days to sale, isn't public. You'd need dealer records or direct confirmation from past sellers.
